Portrait only: 4 points. After hint 1: 3. After hint 2: 2. After hint 3: 1.

With typed answers on, pick the team, type their guess and press Enter: a correct name scores automatically, and close spellings count. Otherwise, when a team calls out the right name, click that team's award button (or press its number). The answer is revealed and points are added. Use the small +/− buttons to correct a score.

Leaders & photos

Portraits load from Wikimedia Commons, with each leader's Wikipedia photo as a backup. A red status means neither loaded on this computer (often a school web filter); upload your own to fix it. Uploads and custom leaders are saved in this browser.

Move photos between computers Photos are saved in this browser only. Save a photo pack at home, then load it at school.
