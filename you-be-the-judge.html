How to play

  1. Read the facts aloud, then press Space to start the clock.
  2. A new clue unlocks every few seconds. The earlier a team answers, the more it's worth.
  3. A team buzzes in and gives two answers: which amendment is at stake, and was the right violated or not violated?
  4. Press that team's number. Mark each answer right or wrong. A wrong amendment locks that team out and the clock keeps going.
  5. Then reveal the ruling. Real cases show the actual Supreme Court decision.

Scoring: right amendment = 4 pts with facts only, 3 after clue 1, 2 after clue 2, 1 after clue 3. Right verdict adds +2.
